## 💡 Tips for Best Results

1. **Be specific**: Include origin, destination, and budget for best results
   - ✅ Good: "Flights from SFO to Miami under $500"
   - ❌ Vague: "Show me flights"

2. **Use airport codes or city names**: Both work!
   - ✅ "SFO to Miami" or "San Francisco to Miami"
   - ✅ "BOM to DEL" or "Mumbai to Delhi"

3. **Include budget**: Helps the agent find deals within your price range
   - ✅ "Under $500" or "Budget $1000"

4. **Specify dates or timeframes**:
   - ✅ "This weekend"
   - ✅ "Next week"
   - ✅ "October 25-27"
   - ✅ "Next month"

5. **Add preferences**: The agent understands constraints like:
   - `pet-friendly`
   - `near-transit` or `near airport`
   - `luxury`
   - `direct` or `nonstop`
   - `no red-eye`
   - `beachfront`
   - `downtown`

## 🎯 Intent Keywords

The agent recognizes these keywords to understand what you want:

- **Search/Find**: `find`, `search`, `show`, `book`, `trip`, `weekend`, `vacation`, `go to`, `departure`, `from`
- **Watch/Track**: `track`, `watch`, `alert`, `notify`, `monitor`
- **Rate Comparison**: `is this good`, `is this rate good`, `good deal`, `worth it`
- **Booking**: `book`, `quote`, `reserve`, `confirm`
- **Refinement**: `make it`, `add`, `also`, `and`, `plus`
- **Policy Questions**: `refund`, `cancel`, `pet`, `breakfast`, `fee`, `neighborhood`, `alternative`

## 📊 What the Agent Returns

When you search, the agent will:

1. **Parse your request** - Extract origin, destination, budget, dates, preferences
2. **Search databases** - Query AI database (1,004+ flight deals, 581+ hotel deals) and MySQL (10,015+ flights, 632+ hotels, 18,497+ cars)
3. **Create bundles** - Combine flights + hotels + cars into travel packages
4. **Show summaries** - Present 2-3 best bundle options with prices and details

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**If the agent asks for clarification:**
- Provide the missing information (origin, destination, or budget)
- The agent will remember your previous messages in the conversation

**If no results found:**
- Try a different origin/destination
- Increase your budget
- Remove some constraints
- The agent will offer to set up a watch to monitor for deals

**If you want to refine:**
- Say "make it pet-friendly" or "add near transit"
- Say "also include car rental"
- The agent will update your search
